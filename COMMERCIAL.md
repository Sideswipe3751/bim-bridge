## When to purchase a commercial license

A separate paid commercial license is required for use of the post-transition
code outside the permissions of PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 or another valid
license. Examples ordinarily include a commercial enterprise's production BIM
work, paid BIM consulting or project delivery, commercial software integration,
and providing commercial hosted automation services. These examples remain
subject to the license's express permissions, including the organization
provision above, and applicable statutory rights. They do not restrict
previously granted Apache-2.0 rights or third-party licenses.
